New Patients

Your first visit

If your doctor referred you, or you want to be seen for a heart or vascular concern, start with a phone call.

Call 717-844-6369
  • Call to schedule Call 717-844-6369. Let us know which office is more convenient.
  • Send your records Ask your referring office to fax notes, test results, and the referral to 717-844-6429.
  • Bring to your visit Photo ID, insurance card, and a current list of your medications.
  • Arrive a little early This leaves time to check in and complete new patient forms.
